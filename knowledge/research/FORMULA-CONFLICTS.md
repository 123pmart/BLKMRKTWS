# Formula Version Reconciliation

The repository contained an older product guide and newer current-label assets with materially different formulas. The 2026-07-27 canonical build uses the current labels.

## DEFY

Current label: 150 mg Eria Jarensis and 50 mg Mucuna Pruriens at two scoops. The older guide showed 300 mg and 150 mg. The current label wins.

## RULE

Current label: 100 mg Mucuna Pruriens and 80 mg Bitter Orange Extract at two scoops. The older guide showed 350 mg and 100 mg. The current label wins.

## UNDERGROUND

Current label: 1,000 mg taurine, 600 mg Dendrobium 10:1, and 350 mg Caffeine Anhydrous. The older guide showed a different focus block, including 1,000 mg L-Tyrosine, 800 mg Dendrobium, and 300 mg caffeine. The current label wins.

## NOOTROPIC

Current label: 600 mg Alpha GPC 50%, 500 mg Lion’s Mane, and the current focus/performance blocks represented in the canonical export. Older guide values are not merged into the current record.

## CUTS Thermogenic Whey Protein Isolate

The current official product page lists 25 g whey protein isolate, L-Carnitine Tartrate, Chromium Picolinate, 15 servings, Chocolate and Vanilla, and marks both variants sold out/coming soon. It is not in the wholesale portal catalog, and no readable official Supplement Facts panel was found in the repository. It remains a documented source gap instead of being promoted to a formula record: the assistant must not infer a serving weight, complete ingredient list, stimulant status, directions, or wholesale availability from marketing copy alone.

This document exists so future updates do not accidentally reintroduce superseded formulas.
