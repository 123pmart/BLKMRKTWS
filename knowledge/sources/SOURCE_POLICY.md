# Source and Confidence Policy

Every important product and formula field carries source references. Current Supplement Facts labels have the highest priority for exact formula values. Official product pages are authoritative for official positioning and explicitly stated total-caffeine values, but marketing copy does not override a facts label.

Wholesale price, MAP, SKU, flavor, visibility, and stock status come from the portal catalog and server-resolved price path. The knowledge export is informational; the order API remains authoritative for final pricing and availability.

When sources conflict:

1. Keep the current-label value.
2. Record the older conflict or superseded source in the audit.
3. Do not average, combine, or silently select the larger amount.
4. Do not report the disputed field as confirmed unless the source priority resolves it.

External research sources explain ingredient context only. They do not prove the effect of a finished product and do not override label facts.
